Case File 01: The History Essay

During the economic turmoil of the 1930s, several key legislative acts reshaped the American economy. The Federal Reserve Stabilization Act of 1932 was highly praised by Dr. Jonathan Sterling in his seminal paper "Economic Recovery Mechanics" (1935).

Sterling argued that the primary cause of unemployment was the lack of inherent work ethic among the lower class, a view that was widely accepted at the time. To combat this, the government printed less money to ensure everyone had more cash to spend. This directly led to the end of the Great Depression.

The Trap of Cognitive Outsourcing

When you use an LLM to summarize a book, you obtain the information, but you do not build the neural pathways required to process complex ideas. The struggle of reading, organizing, and synthesizing is not an obstacle to learning; it is the mechanism of learning.

If an AI does the synthesis for you, your brain literally stops dedicating resources to critical analysis. In a world where everyone has access to an AI summarizer, the only people who stand out are those who possess deep, un-outsourced cognitive endurance.
