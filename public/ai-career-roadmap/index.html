Step-by-Step AI Career Timeline

Stage 1: High School (Class 10–12)

Foundations in Mathematics & Logic

Focus on Mathematics (Linear Algebra, Matrix Calculus, Probability, and Statistics). Learn basic Python programming logic, data structures, and algorithmic thinking.

Stage 2: Undergraduate College (Years 1–2)

Core CS & Traditional Machine Learning

Master Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), SQL databases, Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy, and basic regression/classification algorithms.

Stage 3: Advanced College (Years 3–4)

Deep Learning, LLMs & Generative AI

Dive deep into PyTorch, Neural Networks (CNNs, Transformers), RAG architecture, Vector Databases (Pinecone/Chroma), Fine-tuning (LoRA), and LangChain.

Stage 4: Industry Entry & Placement

Portfolio Projects, Internships & MLOps

Deploy end-to-end production AI apps using Docker, FastAPI, and AWS. Participate in Kaggle competitions, publish open-source GitHub repos, and clear AI technical interviews.

Stage 5: Career Growth & Leadership (2026–2035)

Senior AI Specialist, Architect & Entrepreneurship

Scale enterprise AI systems, direct multi-agent AI workflows, lead engineering teams, or launch your own AI-native SaaS startup.
