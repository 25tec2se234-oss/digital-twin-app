The Higher Ed Challenge

The Three Structural Blind Spots in Campus Placements

Academic excellence alone is no longer enough to clear modern corporate engineering filters. Here is where existing placement workflows struggle:

1. The Black Box of Readiness

Training & Placement Officers (TPOs) typically discover student skill deficits only after campus recruitment rejection rates spike. Spreadsheets and attendance logs do not provide predictive insight into whether students can pass coding screens.

2. Credentials vs. Verifiable Proof

Passive course completion certificates carry diminishing credibility with hiring managers. Recruiters look for live GitHub repositories, system architecture trade-offs, and verified project output over paper certificates.

3. Accreditation Outcome Mandates

Regulatory frameworks (including NAAC Criteria 2.6 and NIRF graduate outcome matrices) increasingly demand verifiable evidence of program learning outcomes and measurable industry alignment across batches.

Pedagogical Framework

The 6-Step Verifiable Career-Readiness Loop

DTV connects student effort to measurable progress through an explainable, practical engineering workflow:

1

Target Role Selection

The student commits to a specific technical career track (Full-Stack Web Development, Data Analytics, Cloud & DevOps). Requirements match modern industry job descriptions rather than generic academic syllabi.

2

Diagnostic Skill Assessment

A 15-question conceptual and scenario-based diagnostic assessment measures competence across core domains. The system outputs an explainable baseline score and highlights critical skill deficits.

3

Personalized 4-Week Roadmap

A structured sequence of 8 milestone tasks is automatically assembled. The roadmap prioritizes the student's highest-severity skill gaps, preventing aimless tutorial looping.

4

Practical Task Execution

Students build real software artifacts—such as REST APIs, SQL analytical queries, and container configurations—with explicit evaluation rubrics and estimated hours invested.

5

Verifiable Evidence Submission

Students submit live code repositories, documentation, or verifiable outputs. The system checks proof of work prior to marking steps completed, ensuring zero fabricated progress.

6

Reassessment & Delta Measurement

Students reassess their skills after completing roadmap tasks. The system plots pre-training versus post-training skill deltas, providing concrete evidence of learning velocity.

Implementation & Governance

Enterprise Security, Zero Campus IT Overhead

Designed to integrate smoothly without campus server provisioning, complex software installations, or data sovereignty risks:

Turnkey Roster Onboarding

Upload a standard CSV roster containing student names, college emails, and roll numbers. Students receive a secure invite link to start immediately. No local servers or IT intervention needed.

Student Data Sovereignty

Aligned with India's DPDP Act and international student privacy principles. Students own their personal portfolios. Institutional dashboards display aggregate readiness metrics with student consent.

Strict Tenant Sandboxing

Strict multi-tenant authorization prevents cross-institution data bleed. Organization members can only query records explicitly belonging to their accredited tenant boundary.
