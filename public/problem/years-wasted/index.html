🔍 Deep Dive Analysis

1. The Blind Choice Trait

Students select degrees based on peer influence, family expectation, or outdated societal perception. Without hands-on simulation of what a role (e.g. Data Engineer, AI Product Manager, UI Designer) actually entails day-to-day, enthusiasm evaporates within semesters.

2. High Sunk-Cost Dilemma

By the time a student realizes they dislike coding or management finance in year 3, shifting streams is heavily penalized with lost tuition, delayed entry into the workforce, and societal pressure.
