🔍 Deep Dive Analysis

1. Social Comparison & Hyper-Competitive Environment

Students are constantly exposed to competitive rank lists, coaching institute billboards, and social media success stories. This fosters a fear of missing out (FOMO) and leads students to pursue careers for prestige rather than aptitude.

2. Communication Gap Between Parents and Students

Parents often recommend careers based on the job market of 20 years ago (e.g. Govt exams, core engineering), whereas today's high-growth fields include AI Engineering, Product Management, Growth Marketing, and UI/UX Architecture.
