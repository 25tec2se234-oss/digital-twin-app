Kumar Kartikey

Founder, Digital Twin Verse

The Vision

Kumar Kartikey is the Founder of Digital Twin Verse (DTV), an innovative ed-tech startup built on the premise that no student should guess their future. With a deep passion for artificial intelligence and a relentless drive to reform career counseling, Kumar conceptualized DTV to provide students with a deeply personalized, simulation-based career mapping experience.

Pioneering AI in Education

Recognizing the systemic gap between traditional education systems and the rapidly evolving demands of the modern workforce, Kumar engineered the foundation of Digital Twin Verse. The platform utilizes a multi-agent AI architecture to create a "Digital Twin" of each student, enabling predictive career simulations, skill gap analysis, and personalized roadmaps that adapt in real time to the student's progress and interests.

Mission at Digital Twin Verse

As the visionary behind DTV, Kumar's mission is to democratize high-quality, actionable career guidance across India. By leveraging advanced machine learning models and immersive virtual experiences, he is dedicated to transforming how the next generation discovers their true potential, builds critical skills, and ultimately secures a successful and fulfilling career path in a technology-first world.
